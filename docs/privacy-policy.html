WearWallet Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 6, 2026

Last Updated: March 8, 2026

Summary: WearWallet is a non-custodial cryptocurrency wallet. We do NOT store, access, or transmit your private keys, seed phrases, or wallet passwords. Your cryptographic keys never leave your device.

1. Introduction

WearWallet ("we", "our", "the app") is a non-custodial cryptocurrency wallet application designed for Wear OS devices, developed by Yushang Lung. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our application.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Private Keys & Seed Phrases: Your wallet's private keys and mnemonic seed phrases are generated and stored exclusively on your device. We never have access to them.
  • Wallet Passwords: Your wallet encryption password is stored locally on your device only.
  • Transaction Details: We do not store or log your transaction history on our servers.
  • Personal Identification: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personally identifiable information.

3. Information We Collect

We collect minimal, non-personal information to improve app stability and performance:

  • Crash Reports (Firebase Crashlytics): Anonymous crash logs including device model, OS version, and stack traces to help us fix bugs. No personal data is included.
  • Performance Data (Firebase Performance): Anonymous app performance metrics (startup time, network latency) to optimize user experience.
  • Analytics (Firebase Analytics): Anonymous usage patterns (screens visited, features used) to improve the app. No wallet addresses or financial data is tracked.

4. Blockchain Data

To display wallet balances and facilitate transactions, WearWallet connects to public blockchain nodes (via Infura, public RPC endpoints, and similar providers). These connections transmit your public wallet address to retrieve balance information. Public wallet addresses are inherently public on the blockchain.

5. AI Features

WearWallet includes optional AI assistant features powered by Google's Gemini API. When you use AI features:

  • Your queries are sent to Google's servers for processing.
  • No wallet private keys, seed phrases, or passwords are ever sent to AI services.
  • AI interactions are subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Storage & Security

  • All sensitive data (private keys, seed phrases, passwords) is encrypted using Android's Keystore system and stored exclusively on your device.
  • Database encryption is provided via SQLCipher.
  • We employ industry-standard security practices including Firebase App Check device integrity verification.

7. Third-Party Services

WearWallet uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google) — Crashlytics, Performance, Analytics, App Check
  • Infura — Blockchain node access
  • Google Gemini AI — AI assistant features
  • Rango Exchange — DEX aggregation for token swaps

8. Children's Privacy

WearWallet is not intended for use by individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with information, please contact us.

9. Your Rights Under GDPR (European Economic Area)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right of Access: You may request information about the data we process about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request deletion of your data. Since WearWallet stores all personal data locally on your device, uninstalling the app effectively deletes all data.
  • Right to Data Portability: Your wallet's seed phrase allows you to export and import your wallet to any compatible wallet application.
  • Right to Object: You may object to data processing by disabling Firebase Analytics in app settings.

Legal basis for processing: Legitimate interest (app stability and improvement via anonymized crash/performance data).

10. Your Rights Under CCPA (California)

If you are a California resident, under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected (see Section 3 above — we collect only anonymous crash/analytics data).
  • Delete your personal information. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.
  • Opt-Out of the sale of personal information. We do NOT sell any personal information.
  • Non-Discrimination: You will not be discriminated against for exercising your CCPA rights.
